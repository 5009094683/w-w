## About the Role

Join our AI team to build, fine-tune, and deploy large language models and ML pipelines that power FlavorGPT and other AI products.

## Responsibilities

- Build and optimise LLM-based applications (RAG, agents, tool-use).
- Develop evaluation frameworks and prompt engineering strategies.
- Work with vector databases (Pinecone, Weaviate, Cloudflare Vectorize).
- Deploy models on cloud infrastructure with efficient inference pipelines.

## Requirements

- 2+ years working with ML/AI in production environments.
- Experience with Python, PyTorch or TensorFlow, and LLM APIs.
- Understanding of transformer architectures and fine-tuning techniques.
- Familiarity with cloud-based GPU infrastructure.
