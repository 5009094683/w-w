## About the Role

The Business Development Intern role is designed for a highly motivated individual looking to build a foundation in enterprise tech sales. You will work directly alongside our senior business development and sales teams, getting a front-row seat to how enterprise deals are sourced, nurtured, and closed. We don't hire interns to fetch coffee; you will be doing real work that impacts our pipeline. This is a fast-paced learning environment where your curiosity, resilience, and attention to detail will help drive our market expansion.

## What You Will Do

- Conduct deep-dive market research to identify target accounts, key decision-makers, and current industry trends.
- Draft and execute outbound outreach campaigns using email, LinkedIn, and phone to generate initial interest from prospective clients.
- Qualify inbound leads and set up discovery meetings for the senior Business Development team.
- Maintain and organize account data within our CRM (Salesforce/HubSpot) to ensure the sales team has accurate, actionable information.
- Shadow discovery calls and technical demos, gradually taking on more active roles in initial prospect conversations.

## Requirements

- Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Business, Marketing, Economics, Communications, or a related field.
- Exceptional written and verbal communication skills; you know how to write a concise, compelling email.
- A high level of self-motivation, organization, and a genuine interest in building a career in B2B tech sales.
- Comfortable handling rejection and navigating a fast-paced, metric-driven environment.
- Basic proficiency with productivity tools (Google Workspace/Microsoft Office) and a quick learner when it comes to new software (CRMs, sales engagement platforms).
