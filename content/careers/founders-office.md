## About the Role

Operating out of the Founder's Office, you will act as a strategic partner and force multiplier for the CEO. This is a fully remote, high-visibility role where no two days are the same. You will sit at the intersection of strategy and execution, helping to drive company-wide initiatives, manage executive priorities, and ensure seamless communication across all departments. This role requires someone with a highly analytical mind, zero ego, and the ability to bring structure to ambiguity. You will get an unfiltered look at how to scale an enterprise company from the top down.

## What You Will Do

- Spearhead high-priority, cross-functional projects on behalf of the Founder, ensuring they are executed on time and aligned with company goals.
- Conduct vital operational research, analyze business metrics, and prepare comprehensive briefs, investor updates, and board decks.
- Act as a gatekeeper and strategic sounding board for the Founder, prioritizing their time, triaging inbound requests, and resolving bottlenecks before they escalate.
- Facilitate executive leadership meetings, build the agendas, capture key decisions, and relentlessly follow up on action items.
- Draft internal and external communications on behalf of the Founder, maintaining an authentic and professional tone.

## Requirements

- 3+ years of experience in management consulting, venture capital, investment banking, or a Chief of Staff / strategic operations role at a fast-growing tech company.
- Exceptional project management skills with a proven ability to drive multiple complex initiatives to completion simultaneously.
- Strong financial literacy and data analysis skills; you must be highly proficient in Excel/Google Sheets and building presentation decks.
- High emotional intelligence, unquestionable integrity, and the discretion to handle highly sensitive and confidential company information.
- Proven ability to thrive in a fully remote environment, demonstrating proactive communication, self-discipline, and deep accountability.
