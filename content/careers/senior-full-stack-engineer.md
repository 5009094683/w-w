## About the Role

We are looking for a Senior Full-Stack Engineer to help build and scale our core products. You will work across the entire stack — from designing APIs and database schemas to building pixel-perfect UIs.

## Responsibilities

- Architect and implement features end-to-end across frontend and backend.
- Work with Vue/Nuxt, React/Next.js, Node.js, Go, Rust and TypeScript daily.
- Design and optimise PostgreSQL and Redis-based data stores.
- Participate in code reviews and mentor junior engineers.
- Deploy and manage services on Cloudflare Workers, AWS, GCP, Azure, and oracle.

## Requirements

- 4+ years of professional full-stack experience.
- Strong proficiency in TypeScript, Node.js, Go, Rust and at least one modern frontend framework.
- Experience with CI/CD pipelines, containerisation, and cloud platforms.
- Confident communication skills and a bias for shipping.
