## About the Role

As our Technical Business Development Manager, you will serve as the critical bridge between our engineering capabilities and our enterprise clients' business needs. Unlike a traditional sales role, this position requires a deep, hands-on understanding of complex technical architectures. You will partner with IT leaders, CTOs, and engineering leads to uncover their technical pain points and demonstrate exactly how our solutions integrate into their existing tech stacks to drive ROI. You are a consultant first and a closer second, trusted by technical buyers because you speak their language.

## What You Will Do

- Drive the entire enterprise sales cycle from initial technical discovery to proof-of-concept (POC) design, negotiation, and closing.
- Lead technical scoping sessions with prospective clients to evaluate their infrastructure, identify security/integration requirements, and map out viable solutions.
- Translate highly technical product capabilities into clear business value and revenue-driving narratives for non-technical stakeholders.
- Collaborate closely with our product and engineering teams, bringing actionable market feedback to help shape the product roadmap.
- Build and manage a robust pipeline of high-value enterprise accounts, maintaining accurate forecasting and account data in the CRM.

## Requirements

- 5+ years of experience in enterprise business development, technical sales, or solutions engineering.
- A strong technical foundation (e.g., a degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent hands-on experience in software development, cloud architecture, or systems integration).
- Proven track record of consistently meeting or exceeding revenue targets in a complex, multi-stakeholder B2B environment.
- Ability to clearly explain complex technical concepts to both engineering teams and C-level executives.
- Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with a high degree of comfort delivering technical demonstrations.
