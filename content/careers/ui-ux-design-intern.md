## About the Role

We are looking for a UI/UX Design Intern who is passionate about creating beautiful and intuitive interfaces. You will work closely with our product and engineering teams to design user experiences for web and mobile products.

## What You Will Do

- Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity mockups in Figma.
- Conduct user research and usability testing.
- Collaborate with developers to ensure design fidelity in production.
- Contribute to and maintain our design system.

## Requirements

- Portfolio demonstrating strong visual and interaction design skills.
- Proficiency in Figma or Sketch.
- Basic understanding of HTML/CSS is a plus.
- Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Design, HCI, or related field.
