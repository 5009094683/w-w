## 1. Introduction

Welcome to YBX Labs (formerly YesbhautikX). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with our proprietary products, engage our enterprise engineering services, or visit our website. Because we build high-performance infrastructure across AI, Web3, and enterprise architecture, we treat data privacy and security as fundamental engineering requirements, not just legal obligations.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide, secure, and improve our technology solutions.

- **Account and Contact Data:** Name, email address, billing information, and company details provided when you engage our custom services or subscribe to our products.
- **Enterprise Client Data:** The inputs, configurations, and data payloads processed through our AI, Machine Learning, and Web3 architectures on your behalf.
- **System and Usage Data:** Telemetry, server logs, crash reports, and performance metrics generated when you interact with our platforms.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to operate our business and deliver reliable technology:

- To deploy, maintain, and support our software products and custom infrastructure.
- To audit backend systems, ensure multi-threaded performance, and maintain bulletproof deployment pipelines.
- To communicate essential updates, security advisories, and administrative information.

**A Note on Artificial Intelligence:** When you utilize our enterprise AI and ML solutions, your proprietary data, queries, and contextual workflows remain yours. We do not use private enterprise client data to train public foundational models.

**A Note on Web3:** If you utilize our Web3 and crypto-finance tooling, please be aware that data and transactions broadcasted to public blockchains are by design public, decentralized, and immutable. We cannot alter or delete data that has been permanently written to a public ledger.

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We are in the business of engineering, not data brokering. We do not sell your personal or enterprise data. We only share information under the following conditions:

- **Infrastructure Partners:** With trusted third-party service providers who host our servers, manage our databases, or facilitate secure payments. These partners are bound by strict enterprise confidentiality agreements.
- **Legal and Regulatory Requirements:** If required to do so by law, subpoena, or strict regulatory frameworks governing enterprise technology.
- **Business Transfers:** In connection with a merger, acquisition, or restructuring of YBX Labs.

## 5. Data Security

We implement rigorous, enterprise-grade security protocols. This includes encrypted data transit, secure isolated environments, automated threat scanning, and continuous architectural audits. While we engineer our systems to the highest standards, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Your Rights and Controls

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

- Request a clear record of the personal data we hold about you.
- Request corrections to inaccurate information or deletion of your personal data.
- Restrict or object to certain types of data processing.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

As our product lineup expands and global data regulations evolve, we may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify active enterprise clients of any material changes directly via email or our service dashboards.
