## How billing works

How you are billed depends on what you buy. This summary follows our [Payment & Refund Policy](/refund-policy), which is the authoritative text.

| What you buy | How it is billed | When payment is due |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Custom engineering and architecture services | Typically by milestone or on a retainer, with a payment schedule in your Statement of Work | Within Net 30 days of the invoice date, unless otherwise specified |
| Proprietary products and SaaS | A recurring subscription, monthly or annual | Upfront, before access or deployment |

## Payment methods

We accept standard enterprise payment methods, including major credit cards and bank or wire transfers. For specific contracts, and subject to prior mutual agreement, we may also accept select cryptocurrency payments such as USDC and USDT.

## Refunds and cancellations

- Payments for completed engineering milestones, consulting hours or finalized architectural plans are non-refundable.
- If you end a project early, you are billed for the work completed up to the cancellation date, and any prepaid amount beyond that is refunded.
- Software licenses, API access and deployed infrastructure are not refunded once provisioned. You can cancel a subscription at any time; it ends at the close of the current billing cycle, without a prorated refund.

The full terms, including late fees and billing disputes, are in the [Payment & Refund Policy](/refund-policy).
