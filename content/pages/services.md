## AI and machine learning

AI runs through much of our work. We build, deploy, and manage context-aware artificial intelligence workflows and large-scale model integrations tailored to your specific data and operational needs. This includes AI workflow integration and model training, and it often builds on our own products, [FlavorAI](/products/flavorai) and the [FlavorAI API](/products/flavorai-api).

To discuss an AI project, [contact us](/contact-us) or start with [consulting](/services/consulting).

## How an engagement works

1. **You tell us what you need.** Share your project idea and vision through the [contact form](/contact-us), by email or on a call.
2. **You get a tailored plan.** It covers scope, timeline and cost.
3. **We agree a Statement of Work.** It sets out the specific deliverables, timelines and payment schedule. Custom engineering work is typically billed by milestone or on a retainer.
4. **We do the work in the open.** You get real-time progress dashboards and weekly syncs.
5. **You own the result.** Upon full payment, you own the final custom deliverables defined in your Statement of Work.

The governing texts are our [Terms & Conditions](/terms-conditions) and [Payment & Refund Policy](/refund-policy).
