## What a corporate workshop is

A corporate workshop is training delivered to one company's team, on that team's schedule and around that team's work. Unlike a public course, the examples and exercises can come from the systems the team actually builds, and questions can be about real problems.

Get your team up to speed with our experts in modern technologies. Our goal is to change the way we approach knowledge, learning and education through hands-on training tailored to your needs. Have a YBX Labs team member come to you, with guided workshops on a wide variety of topics.

## Topics

Each workshop is tailored, so there is no fixed catalog. The areas YBX Labs works in every day are the natural starting points:

- [AI and machine learning](/products), including working with large language models
- [Web3 and blockchain](/services/web3)
- [Backend architecture](/services/architectural-planning)
- [Development practices](/services/development)
- [Open-source software](/services/open-source)

## Who it is for

- Engineering teams adopting a technology they have not used before.
- Companies that want a shared baseline of knowledge across a team.
- Teams that learn better by doing than by reading.

## How to prepare

A workshop goes further when three things are decided beforehand: what the team should be able to do at the end, how much the participants already know, and which of your own systems or problems can be used as examples. Share these when you [contact us](/contact-us) and we will shape the session around them.
