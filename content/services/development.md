## What custom development is

Custom development means writing software for one organization's needs instead of adapting a ready-made product. It is the right choice when the product itself is the business, when existing tools do not fit the way a team works, or when several systems have to be joined into one.

A custom project usually covers four layers: the interface people use (web or mobile), the backend that holds the business logic, the database, and the cloud setup that runs it all. YBX Labs works across all four, so one team is responsible for the whole result.

## What we offer

### Custom development

- **Frontend:** React, Angular, Vue.js, Flutter, Swift (iOS), Kotlin (Android)
- **Backend:** Node.js, Python (Django, Flask), Ruby on Rails, Go, PHP (Laravel)
- **Databases:** PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, Firebase, Cassandra
- **Cloud and DevOps:** AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD pipelines

### Project management

- **Planning:** Roadmap creation, scope definition and resource allocation.
- **Execution:** Agile workflows (Scrum or Kanban), sprint planning and daily standups.
- **Delivery:** Strict deadlines, budget tracking and risk mitigation.
- **Support:** Post-launch maintenance, updates and scalability tweaks.

## Who it is for

- Companies that need a new app, a system upgrade or a complex platform and want one team to carry it from idea to launch.
- Teams whose own engineers are at capacity and who want extra people working inside their existing process.
- Organizations with an outdated system that has to be modernized without stopping the business.

## What we don't do

To keep quality high, we avoid:

- Java (Spring, Hibernate and similar)
- .NET (C#, ASP.NET and similar)
- Projects where the founders do not have a clear vision or enough confidence in it. We work with selected clients only.

## Why choose us

- **No lock-in:** You own your code, data and infrastructure.
- **Flexible collaboration:** Work with our team hourly, per project or long term.
- **Transparency:** Real-time progress dashboards and weekly syncs.
- **Future-proofing:** We build with tools that scale as you grow.
