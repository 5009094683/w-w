## What open-source management is

Open-source software is code that anyone may use, study and change under the terms of its license. Almost every modern system is built on it. Open-source management is the work of choosing which projects to rely on, checking their security and license terms, and keeping them up to date once they are in use.

Harness the power of the open-source community, secured for the enterprise. Open-source software accelerates development, but integrating it into an enterprise environment requires strict governance, security auditing and long-term maintenance planning. YBX Labs acts as your architectural partner to safely leverage the open-source ecosystem.

## What we do

- **Strategic implementation:** We evaluate, select and integrate the right open-source tools that align with your existing tech stack and business objectives.
- **Security auditing and compliance:** We rigorously scan open-source dependencies for vulnerabilities and ensure all licensing complies with your enterprise legal standards.
- **Custom modifications:** When off-the-shelf open-source tools don't quite fit, our engineering team modifies and extends the source code to meet your exact operational requirements.
- **Ongoing maintenance:** We manage version updates, patch security flaws and ensure your open-source infrastructure remains stable and reliable year after year.

## Who it is for

- Companies that depend on open-source software and have no one responsible for tracking its security and licenses.
- Teams choosing between several open-source tools for an important part of their system.
- Organizations that need a change to an open-source project and do not have the engineers to make and maintain it.
